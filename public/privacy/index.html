privacytermssupport

Pocofoto is a private photo-sharing app for two people. This notice explains what we collect, why we use it, and what happens when you delete your account.

information we collect

  • account data: your Google or Apple sign-in identifier, email address (including an Apple private relay address), display name, and optional profile photo.
  • shared content: photos, thumbnails, captions, likes, timestamps, and the IDs needed to show that content to the members of your couple.
  • notifications: an optional device push-token and device identifier when you enable notifications.
  • diagnostics: crash and error information from Sentry to help us keep the app working. We do not use crash diagnostics for advertising.
  • optional analytics: product events from Firebase Analytics, PostHog, and Amplitude only after you turn Analytics on in the app. Analytics is off by default and can be withdrawn in Profile.

how we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, pair you with your person, store and deliver your shared photos, send notifications you enable, prevent abuse, respond to reports, and diagnose crashes. We do not sell your information and we do not use your photos for advertising.

who can see shared photos

Shared photos are available only to the members of their couple. A photo you upload remains in the shared couple photo history if you delete your account, so the remaining person does not lose shared memories. Your account, private records, device registrations, and sign-in access are deleted. We do not delete the couple photo tree as part of account deletion.

retention and deletion

You can start account deletion from Profile inside the app. Deletion removes your authentication account, profile, private records, notification registrations, contact records, and pending pairing artifacts. Reports may be retained in a de-identified form for safety and legal records. Shared couple photos and their captions remain available to the remaining couple member.

your choices

You can turn optional analytics off at any time in Profile, disable notifications in device or app settings, and request help at pocofoto.com.tr/support.

contact

For privacy questions, account deletion help, or data concerns, email support@pocofoto.com.tr.
