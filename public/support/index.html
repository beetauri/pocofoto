contact us

Email support@pocofoto.com.tr with the email on your account, a short description, and screenshots when useful. Never send a password, sign-in code, or private key.

report a photo

In the app, open the shared photo and choose Report. Pick the closest reason: abuse, harassment, sexual content, threats, or other. Reports go to our protected moderation queue so the couple cannot edit or delete the report from the client.

block someone

Open Profile and choose Block and end pairing. Blocking ends the active pairing, keeps the shared photo history intact, and prevents the blocked person from pairing with you again through the app.

delete your account

Open Profile → Delete account. This removes your sign-in account, profile, private records, notification registrations, contact records, and pending pairing artifacts. Shared couple photos remain for the other person by design.

urgent safety concerns

Pocofoto support is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first, then send us the report details.
