privacytermssupport

By using Pocofoto, you agree to use it respectfully and to these terms. Pocofoto is a private sharing space for you and one person you choose to pair with.

your account

Keep your sign-in secure and use only an account you control. You may delete your account from Profile. Account deletion removes your account and private records but intentionally preserves the shared couple photo history for the remaining person.

your content

You keep ownership of the photos and captions you upload. You give Pocofoto permission to store, process, and display that content only as needed to provide the service to your couple. Do not upload content you do not have permission to share.

safe use

Do not use Pocofoto to threaten, harass, sexually exploit, impersonate, or harm someone. Do not upload illegal content, malware, or content that violates another person’s privacy. We may remove unsafe captions, restrict accounts, end pairings, or preserve reports when needed to protect people and the service.

reporting and blocking

Use the Report control on a shared photo for abuse, harassment, sexual content, threats, or another safety concern. You can block a person from Profile; blocking ends the active pairing and prevents future pairing attempts in either direction. See Support for help.

availability

We work to keep Pocofoto available and secure, but the service may change or be interrupted for maintenance, outages, or safety reasons.

contact

Questions or safety reports can be sent to support@pocofoto.com.tr.
